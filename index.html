Learning experiences, built end to end.

Close to twenty years in education, from the classroom to curriculum leadership to co-founding Kula Learn. I design learning built around people, not the technology. The people who understand learning should be the brains driving edtech, not the other way around. And I build it all myself: the curriculum, the slide decks and the facilitator guides.

Leading with Influence

An interactive simulation for adults who have to get things done through people who don't report to them.
Six weeks, four people, six moves. Each one responds to a different approach, drawn from Goleman's six leadership styles, and what works on one is actively damaging to another. It never tells you your style. It shows you what happened to four people and lets you draw the conclusion.

Leadership Foundations

Frontline leadership training set on the plant floor, built with AMPC.
Leadership training usually hands you a framework in a classroom, far from the moment you actually need it. This drops you onto the floor to make the call first, then teaches the skill, so it holds when your heart rate is up and someone is waiting for an answer.
